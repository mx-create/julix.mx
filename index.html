About the label

Two founders. One independent label.

Julix&Mx is a record label and company based in the UK. It holds the music and the rights, and it is the home for everything the two founders make together.

Julix brings the performance and the audience. MX brings the production and the technical work. Together they release club-ready records with a South Indian identity.

Co-founder · Artist

Julix (Julia)

UK-based DJ, producer, drummer and content creator. She started on drums at 12 and now plays clubs, resorts and cafés across the UK and Ireland. Recognised at the CS Global Malayali Awards 2026.

Julix is a UK-based DJ, producer and drummer who blends techno, house and EDM with the rhythms and melodies of her Kerala roots. She co-owns the independent label Julix&Mx and plays clubs, resorts and festivals across the UK and Ireland.
